# Buffett lens

Inputs: `./mlab lens buffett TICKER` (checklist + DCF on owner earnings), `./mlab dd TICKER`.
1. Moat in the numbers: ROIC >= 15% through the cycle, stable gross margin, pricing power (margin held through inflation).
2. Owner earnings = FCF − SBC; yield vs 10y Treasury; growth runway.
3. Capital allocation: buybacks below intrinsic value, debt discipline, acquisitions' returns.
4. Margin of safety: market cap vs DCF bear/base/bull. A great business at a bad price is a watchlist entry with a buy level, not a buy.
5. What would make it a permanent loss (disruption, leverage, regulation)?
Timing: technicals-desk for the entry zone (accumulate at support / after earnings flush). Horizon: position (months-years).
Output: quality verdict, intrinsic range, buy-below price, then the trade card.
