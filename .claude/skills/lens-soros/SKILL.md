# Soros lens

Inputs: `./mlab lens soros`, `./mlab macro --liquidity`, `./mlab cot-extremes`, IG client sentiment.
Questions to answer, each with numbers:
1. What is the prevailing bias (the story the market believes)? Evidence: trend stage, flows, positioning, sentiment.
2. Is price reinforcing the fundamentals (reflexive boom: rising price -> easier financing/higher earnings -> more buying)?
   Or has the trend broken while the story still holds (the bust starting)?
3. Where is the flaw in the prevailing bias, and what event exposes it (policy meeting, data print, funding stress)?
4. Liquidity tide: net liquidity direction, real yields, USD, credit spreads.
5. Asymmetry: crowded positioning vs the trend = fuel for the reversal; size up only when the thesis is confirmed by price.
Output: the bias, the flaw, the trigger, the instrument, and when to press vs when to be out. Then the trade card.
